# Quickstart

This walks you through the one path that matters most: **describe the AI assistant you want in a single sentence, let the platform build it for you, then chat with it to get work done.** Everything happens in your browser — no coding required.

::: tip Before you start
All you need is an account you can sign in with. If you don't have one yet, ask your administrator for the **URL** and your **username and password**. To get familiar with the interface first, see [Sign In & UI Tour](./first-login.md).
:::

## The three things you'll do

| Step | What you do | Roughly how long |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| 1. Sign in | Open the URL and enter your account to reach the workbench | Half a minute |
| 2. Describe your need | Say, in one sentence, what you want the assistant to do | A minute or two |
| 3. Wait and chat | The platform builds it in the background, then you chat with it | ~20–40 min to build, chat is instant |

Only the first two steps need you at the keyboard — about 5 minutes total. Step 3 runs automatically in the background, so you can go do something else.

## Step 1: Sign in

1. Type the URL your administrator gave you into the browser address bar and press Enter.
2. On the sign-in page, enter your **username** and **password**, then click **Sign In**.
3. Once you're in, you land on the **Dashboard** — your daily starting point.

![dashboard](/images/dashboard.png)

::: warning Can't sign in?
First double-check the URL, username, and password (watch the capitalization). If it says your account is invalid or disabled, contact your administrator to confirm your account is active. See [Sign In & UI Tour](./first-login.md) for more.
:::

## Step 2: Describe the Agent you want in one sentence

The special thing about Nexus-AI is that you **don't build anything yourself** — just state your idea clearly, and the platform works like a professional team to design, write, test, and launch a working AI assistant (called an **Agent**) for you.

1. On the Dashboard, find the **New Agent** entry and open the creation page.
2. In the description box, explain what you need in plain words. The more specific you are, the better the Agent fits your needs.
3. When it looks right, click **Start Build**.

![agents-new](/images/agents-new.png)

A good request usually covers three things: **who it's for, what it should do, and what result you expect**. Compare the examples below:

| Too vague | Better |
|-----------|--------|
| Help me with documents | Create an assistant that reads PDF contracts, extracts key clauses, and lists them out |
| Analyze data | Create an assistant that analyzes the monthly sales spreadsheet, spots gains and drops, and writes a short summary |
| Make a support bot | Create an assistant that answers common customer questions based on our product guide |

::: tip Not sure how to phrase it?
Just tweak one of the examples above. If you're not happy with the result, you can always ask it to adjust later — it doesn't have to be perfect on the first try. See [Creating an Agent](./create-agent.md) for full tips.
:::

## Step 3: Wait for the build to finish

Once you click **Start Build**, the platform starts working in the background. You'll see a **Build Progress** page showing which step it's on.

![project-detail-stages](/images/project-detail-stages.png)

1. The progress page updates step by step until it shows **Build complete**.
2. When it's done, the Agent appears in your Agent list, ready to use.

::: tip How long does it take?
About **20–40 minutes** on average. You don't have to watch the page — feel free to close it, do something else, and come back later. Your progress is kept. For a full read of the progress steps, see [Build Progress](./build-progress.md).
:::

## Step 4: Chat with your Agent

Once the build is done, using your Agent is as easy as chatting.

1. Open the Agent you just built and click **Start Chat**.
2. Type your question or task in the box at the bottom and press Enter.
3. The Agent starts replying, with text appearing piece by piece — that's normal, it means it's answering in real time.

![chat](/images/chat.png)

Try asking it to do one of the things you described at the start and see if the result matches what you expected. If not, just tell it what to change, right there in the chat.

::: tip You can upload files too
During a chat you can upload images, Excel, Word, PDF, and other files so the Agent can use their contents in its answers. See [Chat & Sessions](./chat.md) for more.
:::

## FAQ

**Where is the Agent I built stored? Will I lose it?**
No. It stays in your Agent list and is still there next time you sign in, ready to reuse.

**What if I built the wrong thing or don't want it anymore?**
You can delete it anytime, or ask it to adjust. Building an Agent isn't a one-shot deal — experiment freely.

**What if the build stalls or fails?**
First refresh the progress page to see if it has moved on. If it truly failed, start a new build or describe your need more clearly. If it still won't work, contact your administrator.

**Can I build one even if my description isn't very technical?**
Yes. Describe it the way you'd talk normally — no technical terms needed. That's exactly what the platform is designed for.
